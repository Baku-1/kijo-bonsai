# Design Decision: SLP Dual-Potion System

**Confirmed by Jeremy:** from GDD (v0.2, July 23 2026)
**Status: AUTHORITATIVE**

**Jeremy's clarification — 2026-09-16:** Morale rises gradually while the tree is being cared for. Potions are a quick fix: their recovery is immediate on successful application (after server confirmation of the burn for Ronin SLP). Sustained care can reach 100% without a potion. The 65% ceiling applies to Ronin SLP recovery, not care-based recovery.

---

## Overview

Two potions share the name "SLP." They serve different users, live in different systems, and operate under completely different economic rules. They must never be confused in implementation, documentation, or player communication.

| | Soothing Leaf Potion | Smooth Love Potion |
|---|---|---|
| **User** | Caretaker | Fighter |
| **System** | Server-side in-game item | Ronin blockchain ERC-20 token |
| **Tradeable?** | No | Yes (DEX, marketplace) |
| **On-chain?** | Never | Always |
| **Cost** | Free (crafted from care byproducts) | Market price of Ronin SLP |
| **Morale restoration** | Immediate full restoration (100%) | Immediate partial restoration after confirmed burn (caps at 65%) |
| **Diminishing returns?** | No | Yes |
| **Burned on use?** | N/A (destroyed server-side, never on-chain) | Yes — permanently burned from Ronin supply |

## Soothing Leaf Potion (Caretaker's SLP)

### What It Is

The Soothing Leaf Potion is brewed by caretakers from the natural byproducts of tree maintenance. It is the real cure for a kijo's wounded spirit — the bond between the kijo and her caretaker, expressed through the act of care itself made tangible.

### Ingredients (Collected Through Care)

- **Bark shavings** — from pruning (pruned branch material)
- **Dried petals** — from seasonal leaf-fall (collected passively each autumn)
- **Root clippings** — from repotting (periodic maintenance action)
- **Spring dew** — collected during the first days of each spring cycle

All ingredients come from ordinary care activity. A caretaker who tends their tree naturally generates the components needed to brew potions. No special actions are required — the brewing is a byproduct of good care.

### Properties

- **Account-locked.** The caretaker brews it. The caretaker uses it. Exclusively on trees they are currently delegated to tend (or their own trees). Cannot be passed to another account by any means.
- **Non-tradeable.** Cannot appear on any marketplace, DEX, or trade window.
- **Non-transferable.** Cannot be sent to another wallet, even within the same delegation.
- **Server-side only.** This item exists entirely within the game's backend. It is never minted, never written to the blockchain, never represented as a token.
- **Free to produce.** The ingredients are generated by care — a caretaker who does their job accumulates the materials automatically.

### Effect

Soothing Leaf Potion provides **immediate full morale restoration to 100%** when successfully applied. Offered to the kijo through the tree, it calms the spirit through the care bond and returns her to full fighting willingness.

Sustained care also restores morale gradually to 100%; a potion is not required for full recovery. The potion is the quick fix. The 65% ceiling applies specifically to Smooth Love Potion recovery and must not cap care-based recovery. Existing combat-event gains remain defined in `DESIGN-SPIRIT-MORALE.md`.

## Smooth Love Potion (Fighter's SLP — the Ronin Token)

### What It Is

Smooth Love Potion **IS the Ronin SLP ERC-20 token.** It is not a new token created by Kijo. It is the existing Ronin SLP, already listed on exchanges, already in circulation, already known to the Ronin ecosystem. Kijo's relationship with this token is approved by Sky Mavis and the Ronin team.

Fighters spend real SLP to partially recover their kijo's morale — enough to continue fighting when the kijo's spirit is flagging, but not enough to fully restore her. It is explicitly a band-aid, not a cure. The caretaker remains essential.

### Properties

- **Tradeable** — acquired through Ronin DEX, marketplaces, tournament prizes, or direct purchase.
- **On-chain** — all SLP transactions are standard Ronin blockchain interactions.
- **Burned on use** — the SLP is permanently removed from the Ronin supply via a transfer to a dead address or approved burn mechanism. There is no minting. Kijo only consumes SLP; it never emits it.

### Effect and Limits

- Restores approximately **20–30% morale** per use.
- **Diminishing returns on repeated use** within the same recovery window:
  - First use: ~20–30% morale restored
  - Second use in same window: ~15%
  - Third use in same window: ~8%
- **Morale cap: 65%.** No matter how many SLP are burned, morale cannot exceed 65% through this mechanism. The partial boost is immediate after the server confirms the burn. Full morale (100%) can be restored gradually through sustained care or immediately through the caretaker's Soothing Leaf Potion.
- The diminishing returns and cap are not bugs — they are explicit design to prevent SLP from fully replacing caretaker care.

### Acquisition Channels

- Ronin DEX
- Secondary marketplaces
- Tournament prizes
- Any future Ronin ecosystem channel where SLP is tradeable

Kijo's game server does not distribute SLP to players. Kijo is a sink only.

## Economic Design

### Kijo as an SLP Sink

Kijo does not mint SLP. It only burns it. Every battle loss that triggers morale recovery burns real SLP from the Ronin token supply.

- Demand scales directly with Kijo combat activity — more fights = more losses = more SLP burned
- Diminishing returns prevent brute-force morale farming — eventually you MUST wait for your caretaker or rest the kijo
- The caretaker remains economically essential: Ronin SLP recovery caps at 65%; sustained care restores morale gradually, while Soothing Leaf Potion restores 100% immediately.
- Sky Mavis benefits because Kijo adds real utility demand to SLP without minting new supply
- Kijo benefits because SLP has existing liquidity, exchange listings, and recognition — no cold-start token problem

### Why This Succeeds Where Axie's SLP Failed

In Axie Infinity, SLP was both the faucet (earned by grinding) AND the sink (spent on breeding). When breeding demand dropped, the faucet kept running and SLP inflated to near-zero.

In Kijo, SLP is ONLY a sink — Kijo never emits SLP, only consumes it. The token's supply-side economics are entirely outside Kijo's control (they belong to the Ronin ecosystem). Kijo contributes pure demand. That is the cleanest possible relationship with an existing token.

The structural difference:
- **Axie SLP:** game mints SLP + game burns SLP → equilibrium dependent on internal balance → collapsed when internal balance tilted
- **Kijo SLP:** game does not mint SLP + game burns SLP → pure demand pressure on an external token → cannot create inflation, can only reduce supply

## The Caretaker Dependency (Design Intent)

The 65% morale cap from SLP is not a monetization decision — it is a structural design pillar. It ensures:

1. **Fighters cannot eliminate caretakers from the economy.** No amount of SLP spending fully replaces what a caretaker provides.
2. **The delegation system has permanent value.** A fighter who delegates to a skilled caretaker gains sustained care and access to immediate 100% morale restoration through Soothing Leaf Potion. A fighter relying solely on Ronin SLP for recovery is limited to 65% through that mechanism; a solo player who also tends their own tree can recover fully through care.
3. **The care loop has permanent stakes in combat outcomes.** A neglected tree costs the fighter SLP (from morale recovery) and still can't reach full fighting capacity.

## For Implementers

- The Soothing Leaf Potion must never be minted as an on-chain token, represented as a tradeable asset, or given a blockchain interaction. It is a server-side game item. Any attempt to make it transferable, tradeable, or on-chain is a design violation.
- The Smooth Love Potion interaction must use the actual Ronin SLP ERC-20 contract. The burn mechanism (transfer to dead address or approved burn function) must be confirmed with Sky Mavis before implementation.
- Morale recovery from SLP is server-side: the fighter initiates the SLP transaction, the server confirms the burn on-chain, then credits morale. The credit logic is server-authoritative.
- The diminishing returns window must be defined — "same recovery window" needs a concrete implementation (e.g., a 24-hour window from first SLP use, tracked per tree per fighter).
- The 65% hard cap must be enforced server-side. Client should display the cap clearly to fighters attempting additional SLP use.
- SLP use events should be logged with transaction hashes for verification.
- The ingredient collection system for Soothing Leaf Potion is server-side only. Track: bark shavings (incremented per prune), dried petals (accumulated per autumn game-day), root clippings (per repotting action), spring dew (per spring cycle first-day).

---

## For Auditors

- Soothing Leaf Potion appearing in any marketplace, trade window, or wallet view is a **bug and a design violation**.
- Morale exceeding 65% from SLP use alone (without Soothing Leaf Potion being applied) is a **bug**.
- Diminishing returns not applying on second and third SLP use within the recovery window is a **bug**.
- Any code path that mints or emits SLP from Kijo's contracts is a **critical bug and must not ship**.
- SLP burn transactions that do not record the tree morale credit on the server (i.e., the SLP is burned but morale doesn't change) are a **bug** that must be caught before funds are lost.
- A caretaker receiving Soothing Leaf Potion ingredients from any source other than their own care actions is a **bug** (exploitation vector).
- Soothing Leaf Potion restoring morale above 100% is a **bug** — full morale is the cap.
