Vision

A web3 fighting game where players nurture a bonsai tree harboring the spirit of a Kijo (a fierce dryad), then pit their guardian spirits against each other in intense one-on-one combat. It merges the intimate care mechanics of a digital pet with the competitive depth of a traditional 2D fighting game, creating a unique blend of stewardship and battle.

Genre and Inspirations

Your game draws from Mortal Kombat style 2D traditional fighting games. You're building a frame-perfect combat system where every move, every startup frame, every hit advantage matters. Players will spend serious time in training mode learning frame data, mastering combos, and optimizing meter management. This is competitive fighting game DNA, demanding precision and rewarding practice.

Look and Feel

Traditional Japanese art aesthetic brings elegance and intensity to every frame. The bonsai trees and Kijo spirits evoke delicate nature intertwined with raw power. Expect rich, hand-crafted visuals with the weight and intentionality of classical Japanese painting. The UI will honor this aesthetic without compromising the clarity competitive players demand.

Core Mechanics

Players perform directional command inputs (quarter circles, half circles, charge moves) to execute normals, specials, and supers. A meter system fills through offense and defense, enabling EX moves and devastating super attacks. Hitbox and hurtbox manipulation through crouch, jump, and animation creates moment-to-moment strategy. Matches run best-of-3, with full training mode featuring hitbox visualization and frame data display so players can study and improve continuously.

What Makes It Special

The blockchain bonsai ownership layer creates persistent, meaningful character identity. Your fighter isn't just a temporary loadout in a match. Each player's bonsai spirit is their long-term investment, their companion in battle. This transforms a fighting game from a skill-based arena into something more personal and emotionally anchored. When two bonsai spirits clash, it carries narrative weight.
